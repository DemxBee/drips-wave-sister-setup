# Wave 11: Facts

Stellar Wave Program, Wave 11. Dates, pool and points as known on 18 November 2026. Confirm the pool and each issue's points in the Drips app before applying, because they can change.

## The Wave

- Runs: 18-25 November 2026.
- Pool: $75,000 shared in proportion to points earned. A $100K USDC figure was quoted in a Stellar roundup; the Drips app is the source of truth.
- Points: Trivial 100, Medium 150, High 200.
- Payout: after the Wave ends and a 7-day Compliment window closes. No fixed payout date. Unclaimed rewards can be reallocated.
- Rewards are generally paid in the organiser's token, not necessarily USDC.
- Points do not normally carry over to the next Wave. Points can be adjusted or deducted, and rewards withheld, if the rules are broken.
- KYC by the natural person who uses the account, one account per participant, 18+.

## How to apply

Drips app → Explore → active Stellar Wave → browse issues → Apply, with a short message about your experience. Or find issues on GitHub with the `Stellar Wave` label. Applying is not being assigned; the maintainer accepts one applicant per issue.

Contributor FAQ: https://docs.drips.network/wave/contributors/faq/
How a Wave works: https://docs.drips.network/wave/maintainers/participating-in-a-wave/
